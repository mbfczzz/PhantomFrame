## Final prompt

Create an original premium cinematic visual for PhantomFrame, an AI image and video creation studio. Asset: full-bleed website login hero photograph / photoreal 3D concept art, portrait 4:5 composition, no typography. Scene: a monumental vertical rounded-rectangle portal of polished dark chrome suspended just above still black water in an otherworldly obsidian landscape. Inside the portal, luminous violet clouds and a cool cyan horizon open into a dreamlike world. A tiny solitary human silhouette near the lower center gives a sense of scale. Reflections ripple subtly on the water. The portal is the clear focal point, centrally framed in the lower-middle of the image. Reserve the top quarter as dark atmospheric negative space for a separate HTML brand label; keep lower corners dark enough for a separate HTML caption. Midnight black and charcoal base, electric violet and restrained icy cyan illumination, volumetric haze, finely detailed materials, filmic contrast, believable light, polished cinematic VFX, intriguing and beautiful rather than busy. Elegant composition with one unmistakable subject, nuanced light falloff, photographic depth. No text, no letters, no brand marks, no UI, no borders, no collage, no watermark. This is original marketing concept artwork, not a copy of an existing website.
